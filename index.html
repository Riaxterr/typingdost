SSC CHSL, SSC CGL & RRB NTPC Typing Test Online Prep

Government exam typing tests in India (like SSC CHSL, SSC CGL, RRB NTPC, High Court Clerk, Delhi Police, and state-level exams) require candidates to type with specific speed thresholds and error constraints. On TypingDost, you can practice under realistic exam conditions. Selecting our **10-minute typing test** mimics the exact duration of official tests.

For exams like **SSC CHSL**, candidates are required to type a paragraph of approximately **1750 key depressions (KDPH)** in 10 minutes, which translates to a typing speed of **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)**. The evaluation system checks both full mistakes (like omitting words or replacing characters) and half mistakes (like spelling errors or capitalization issues). Practicing daily on TypingDost builds the muscle memory needed to achieve high accuracy under actual exam pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions — Typing Test & WPM

How can I prepare for government typing tests like SSC CHSL/CGL? +
To prepare for government exams like **SSC CHSL, SSC CGL, or RRB NTPC**: 1. Focus on touch typing using all ten fingers, keeping your eyes on the screen, not the keyboard. 2. Use a standard mechanical keyboard for practice, as laptop keyboards have low travel distances. 3. Practice on TypingDost using the **10-minute timer** and selection of **Govt Exam Mode** paragraphs. 4. Keep your accuracy above 97% to minimize speed penalties due to mistakes.
What is Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH) and WPM conversion? +
**KDPH** stands for Key Depressions Per Hour, which counts every character and space typed in an hour. To convert WPM to KDPH: WPM is calculated assuming 5 key depressions equal 1 word. * **Formula:** KDPH = WPM × 5 × 60. * **Example:** 35 WPM = 35 × 300 = **10500 KDPH** (the minimum speed requirement for SSC CHSL typing exam).
Does TypingDost support Phonetic Hindi typing? +
Yes! TypingDost fully supports smart Phonetic typing. You can type seamlessly using standard English keyboard letters, and it instantly converts to pure Hindi (Devanagari) script with matching sounds and real-time accuracy telemetry. No complex layouts to memorize!
What is a good typing speed in WPM? +
A good typing speed is 50–70 WPM with 95%+ accuracy. Beginners typically type at 30–40 WPM. Professional typists average 70–100 WPM. Competitive typists can exceed 120 WPM. Test your speed for free on TypingDost right now!
What is the average typing speed in words per minute? +
The average typing speed for adults is 40 WPM. For professional office workers, it's 50–60 WPM. Data entry professionals and secretaries are typically required to type at 60–80 WPM.
