About TypingDost

Our Mission

TypingDost ka ek hi mission hai: India ke har student aur professional ko touch typing master karne mein help karna, bilkul FREE. Humara focus un aspirants par hai jo SSC, RRB, aur Court exams ki tayari kar rahe hain aur jinhe high-quality typing resources chahiye.

Who Built TypingDost?

Hi! Main Rizwan Khan hoon, TypingDost ka Lead Developer & Founder. Mujhe coding aur education technology mein deep interest hai.

Maine dekha ki market mein bahot se typing tools either paid hain, unme intrusive ads hote hain, ya phir woh India ke specific competitive exams (jaise SSC CHSL/CGL Mangal font) ko target nahi karte. Is problem ko solve karne ke liye maine TypingDost banaya. Mera vision hai ki education har kisi ke liye accessible ho, regardless of unka background kya hai.

What We Offer

Humare platform par aapko milenge kaafi saare unique features:

  • 14+ Languages: English aur Hindi samet 14 languages me smart Phonetic transliteration typing practice (English type karke regional script generate karein).
  • Free Certificates: Apni speed verify karein aur free me typing certificate generate karein jo aap apne resume me add kar sakte hain.
  • Govt Exam Mock Tests: SSC CHSL, CGL, aur RRB exams ke liye exact exam-like UI/UX aur timer based mock tests.
  • Mechanical Keyboard Sounds: ASMR experience aur rhythm building ke liye mechanical keyboard SFX.
  • Typing Games: Practice ko fun banane ke liye engaging typing mini-games.

Our Impact

TypingDost par aaj rozana hazaaron students aate hain. Kashmir se Kanyakumari tak, aspirants apni speed improve kar rahe hain. Har roz naye typing tests pass kiye jate hain aur certificates issue hote hain. Ye dekhna bahut rewarding hai ki kaise ek simple idea itne logo ki career journey me madad kar raha hai.

Why TypingDost is Different

  • 100% Free Forever: Koi hidden charges ya premium tiers nahi.
  • No Login Required: Bina account banaye direct practice start karein.
  • Privacy-First: Client-side processing ka matlab hai ki aapka data aapke browser tak hi limited rehta hai.
  • Modern Cyberpunk UI: Dark mode by default aur ek immersive typing experience.

Our Expertise (E-E-A-T)

TypingDost sirf ek tool nahi hai. Hum typing education ke field me constant research karte hain. SSC, RRB, aur State Govt exams ke latest notification, error deduction formulas (full mistake / half mistake criteria) aur KDPH requirements ko deeply study karke hum platform ko update karte hain taaki aapko sabse accurate practice environment mile.
