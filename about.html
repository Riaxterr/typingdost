Origin & Mission

About TypingDost

Building India's fastest, zero-login, student-first touch typing practice arena and examination training hub.

🎯 Our Mission

TypingDost ka ek hi mission hai: India ke har student aur professional ko touch typing master karne mein help karna, bilkul FREE. Humara focus un aspirants par hai jo SSC, RRB, aur Court exams ki tayari kar rahe hain aur jinhe high-quality typing resources chahiye.

👨‍💻 Who Built TypingDost?

Hi! Main Rizwan Khan hoon, TypingDost ka Lead Developer & Founder. Mujhe coding aur education technology mein deep interest hai.

Maine dekha ki market mein bahot se typing tools either paid hain, unme intrusive ads hote hain, ya phir woh India ke specific competitive exams (jaise SSC CHSL/CGL, RRB NTPC typing exams) ko target nahi karte. Is problem ko solve karne ke liye maine TypingDost banaya. Mera vision hai ki education har kisi ke liye accessible ho, regardless of unka background kya hai.
