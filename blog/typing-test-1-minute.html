4. The Exact Math: Gross WPM vs Net WPM Formula

Speed check karte waqt sirf fast type karna kafi nahi hai — errors speed ko drastically cut karte hain:

Gross WPM Formula: Total Characters Typed ÷ (5 × Minutes)
Net WPM Formula: Gross WPM − (Uncorrected Errors ÷ Minutes)

Example Calculation: Agar aapne 1 minute mein 300 characters type kiye aur 4 errors hue:

  • Gross Words = 300 ÷ 5 = 60 WPM
  • Net Speed = 60 − 4 = 56 Net WPM
  • Accuracy = ((300 − 20) ÷ 300) × 100 = 93.3%

5. Top 5 Mistakes That Ruin Your 1-Minute Score

  1. Looking Down at the Keys: Keyboard dekhne se eye-tracking break hoti hai aur brain ko word scan karne mein extra 0.5 second lagta hai.
  2. Over-using Backspace: 1 minute mein 10 baar Backspace press karne ka matlab hai 15-20 seconds ka direct loss. Galti hone par smooth flow maintain karein.
  3. Improper Home Row Resting: Index fingers ko hamesha F aur J tactile bumps par wapas aana chahiye.
  4. Tension & Stiff Wrists: Ungliyon ko tight rakhne se speed drop hoti hai. Relaxed fingers fluid movement allow karti hain.
  5. Ignoring Punctuation: Capital letters aur commas par hesitate hona 1-minute test mein common speed killer hota hai.
