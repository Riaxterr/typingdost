1. What is Hindi Phonetic Typing & Why It Is the Future

Phonetic typing ek sound-to-script mapping engine par kaam karta hai. Iska matlab hai ki aap jis sound ko bolte hain, wahi Roman letter press karte hain:

  • k press karne se क banta hai.
  • ka press karne se का banta hai.
  • ki press karne se कि banta hai.
  • kee ya kI press karne se की banta hai.

Kyunki text standard Unicode UTF-8 mein encode hota hai, isliye ise read ya print karne ke liye kisi font file ki zaroorat nahi padti. Yeh har smartphone, tablet, laptop, aur modern government portal par natively support hota hai.
