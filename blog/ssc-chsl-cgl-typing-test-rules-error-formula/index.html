Full Mistakes vs. Half Mistakes: Official SSC Nature of Errors

Advertisement

Unlike casual typing websites that mark single character typos identically, SSC strictly categorizes errors into Full Mistakes (Weight = 1.0) and Half Mistakes (Weight = 0.5) against the master reference passage:

What Counts as 1 Full Mistake?

  • Omission of a word or figure: Skipping any word completely while reading fast.
  • Substitution of a wrong word: Typing affect instead of effect or swapping entire words.
  • Addition of an extra word/figure not printed inside the master passage.

What Counts as a Half Mistake (0.5 Weight)?

  • Spacing errors: Missing a space between two words (inthe) or pressing double space between words (in the).
  • Wrong capitalization: Typing india instead of India or accidentally toggling Caps Lock mid-word.
  • Punctuation & transposition errors: Swapping adjacent letters (teh instead of the) or dropping a comma/period.
  • Spelling / repeated word errors: Typing a minor single-character spelling mistake inside a valid word.

Exact SSC Error Percentage Formula With Worked Example

SSC’s formula divides combined mistake weight by total words (or normalized 5-char word count) in the master passage:

Total Errors = Full Mistakes + (Half Mistakes × 0.5)
Error % = (Total Errors ÷ Total Words in Master Passage) × 100

Worked Example: Suppose an SSC CHSL passage contains 350 words (1,750 keystrokes). Candidate Aman skips 4 words (4 Full Mistakes) and commits 10 spelling/spacing slips (10 Half Mistakes).
Combined Errors = 4 + (10 × 0.5) = 9 Errors.
Error Percentage = (9 ÷ 350) × 100 = 2.57% — comfortably clearing both the strict 5% DEO and 7% UR LDC limits!

Exact Exam Console Workflow on TCS iON Test Day

Understanding what happens on screen during your 25-minute skill-test slot eliminates panic before keystrokes begin:

  • Stage 1 — Hardware Verification Test (1–2 Minutes Warmup): Before the real countdown begins, SSC launches a short mock sample window explicitly to let you test every letter, Spacebar, Shift, Backspace, and Enter key on your seat’s desktop keyboard. Always tap every row firmly and raise your hand to request an immediate keyboard swap if Spacebar squeaks, sticks, or double-triggers!
  • Stage 2 — Break / Instructions Pause (3 Minutes): Relax wrists, take slow breaths, keep your chair tucked cleanly so forearms stay horizontal, and position the keyboard straight—never twisted awkwardly sideways.
  • Stage 3 — Actual 10-Minute / 15-Minute Skill Test: Do NOT rush blindly through paragraph 1 trying to beat the clock. TCS iON Console Simulation Checklist Before Exam Day

  1. Practice uninterrupted 10-minute blocks on TypingDost’s SSC CHSL Simulator and 15-minute blocks on our SSC CGL DEST Simulator.
  2. Beware Skipping Lines: If you skip a whole line of 12 words in a split-screen passage, SSC counts all 12 words as 12 Full Mistakes unless your eyes anchor cleanly line-by-line!
  3. Verify Leftover Seconds: Aim to finish typing 1,750/2,000 depressions with 60–90 seconds left exclusively for proofreading missing spaces or capital letters!

Does Scrolling or Backspace Work Inside the Official Exam?

Yes — current TCS iON consoles allow Backspace within the test duration and provide split/top-bottom passage scrolling, though highlights showing red/green correctness are hidden. Never wait for red flashes to alert you of mistakes; cultivate internal finger awareness above 97% clean accuracy so backspaces stay under 2% of total strokes. If qualifying for Hindi medium LDC or Assistant roles, verify your speed separately on Hindi Typing Test well ahead of Tier-II call letters.

Exemption Rules & Minimum Word Count Caution in SSC Tier-II

Applicants certified under Visual Impairment / Cerebral Palsy OH exemptions receive compensatory 5 extra minutes (or formal exemption where specified inside official SSC Tier-II notices) when submitting verified medical certificates ahead of deadlines. All remaining candidates must remember a vital nuance: failing to complete the full mandatory minimum depression baseline (for example finishing at 1,640 depressions on a 1,750 CHSL passage) disqualifies the module regardless of achieving zero typing mistakes! Frequently Asked Questions

What is the passing error percentage for UR in SSC CHSL vs SSC CGL DEST?

SSC CHSL LDC typically allows up to 7% error for UR (10% Reserved), whereas stricter DEO and Tax Assistant DEST cutoffs allow 5% for UR and 7% for Reserved categories.

Does typing extra words beyond 1,750 key depressions help?

If you finish the displayed master passage early, stop and proofread cleanly! Repeating or typing random extra characters after passage end gets penalized as additions/omissions.

Is 10,500 KDPH equal to 35 WPM?

Yes — 35 words × 5 characters × 60 minutes = 10,500 Key Depressions Per Hour (1,750 keystrokes inside a 10-minute exam).

Are Backspace and Arrow keys permitted during SSC CHSL & CGL typing?

Yes, Backspace and arrow navigation work inside TCS iON typing boxes, though mouse editing may feel clunky — prioritize error-free forward rhythm.
