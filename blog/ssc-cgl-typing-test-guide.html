SSC CGL Typing Test (DEST) 2026: Speed Benchmark, Evaluation Rules & Qualifying Strategy

✍️ By Rizwan Khan (Govt Exam Coach) 📅 Updated: October 2026 ⏱️ 8 Min Read 🏛️ Official SSC Tier-II DEST Format

⚡ Key Takeaways at a Glance

  • Mandatory for All Posts: Under the revised scheme, DEST is compulsory for all qualified candidates in Tier-II.
  • Required Speed: 27 Words Per Minute (WPM), which equals 2000 Key Depressions in 15 Minutes.
  • Error Limit: 5% for General (UR) category; 7% for OBC/EWS; 10% for SC/ST/PwD.
  • Exam Software: Conducted on TCS iON platform with strict on-screen evaluation.

1. Is the SSC CGL Typing Test Mandatory for Everyone?

Yes! Under the revised SSC CGL examination scheme, the Data Entry Speed Test (DEST) is conducted on the very same day as the Tier-II computer-based examination. It is a qualifying examination for all Group B and Group C posts (including Assistant Section Officer in CSS, Tax Assistant in CBDT/CBIC, Postal Assistant, and Divisional Accountant).

Even if you score 340+ marks in Tier-II Paper-1, failing the DEST disqualifies you from post allocation. Therefore, consistent daily practice is vital.

2. SSC CGL DEST Speed & Key Depression Mathematics

The official SSC notification specifies:

  • Total Depressions: 2,000 Key Depressions (characters + spaces).
  • Test Duration: Exactly 15 Minutes.
  • Depressions Per Minute: 2000 ÷ 15 = 133.33 Depressions/Min.
  • Equivalent WPM: Standard word count is 5 keystrokes: 133.33 ÷ 5 = 26.66 WPM ≈ 27 WPM.

4. TCS iON Exam Center Rules & Environment

During the official DEST:

  • You will be given a 2 to 3-minute mock test to verify that every key on the physical keyboard is responsive. If any key sticks or skips, alert the invigilator immediately for a replacement.
  • Backspace is allowed: You can edit mistakes within the passage using arrow keys and the backspace key.
  • Scrolling: The passage is presented in an upper window, and you type in a lower window. You must manually scroll as you progress through lines.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Can I use Backspace in the SSC CGL typing test?
Yes, backspace and arrow navigation are fully enabled in the TCS iON test interface for SSC CGL DEST.
Q: How many WPM do I need to be safe in SSC CGL?
While the requirement is 27 WPM, exam hall nervousness can drop your speed by 5-8 WPM. Target 35+ WPM during practice on TypingDost to be 100% safe.
