5 Steps to Master High-Speed Hindi Typing

  1. Master Home Row Finger Placement: Apni ungliyon ko hamesha A-S-D-F aur J-K-L-; par resting position me rakhein. Jab finger placement solid hoti hai, toh aapki speed bina keyboard dekhe badhti hai.
  2. Practice 15 Minutes Daily: Ek lamba session karne ke bajaye, rozana 15 minute consistent practice karein. Hamare Hindi Typing Test arena par regular drills lagayein.
  3. Focus on Rhythm Over Rush: Typing speed tab aati hai jab aapka rhythm smooth hota hai. Shuru me 98%+ accuracy target karein, speed naturally 30 se 45 WPM cross kar jayegi.
  4. Leverage Your Existing English Typing Skills: Agar aapki English typing speed 35 WPM hai, toh Phonetic typing ki wajah se aap first day se hi Hindi me 30+ WPM achieve kar sakte hain.
  5. Test with Real Passages: Alag-alag topics jaise General Knowledge, Current Affairs aur Story passages par practice karein taaki har type ke shabdon par grip bane.
