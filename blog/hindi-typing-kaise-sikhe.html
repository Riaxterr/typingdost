1. Mangal (Remington Gail) vs Kruti Dev 010: Kaunsa Pehle Sikhein?

Comparison Factor Mangal (Remington Gail / Inscript) Kruti Dev 010

3. Matras & Half-Letters (आधे अक्षर) Ke Rules

  • Halant Logic: Kisi bhi akshar ke baad D (Halant) press karne par wo akshar aadha ho jaata hai. Jaise: d (्) + ; (य) = क्य.
  • Chhoti 'i' Matra (ि): Remington Gail me hamesha akshar type karne ke baad nahi, balki pehle F key dabai jaati hai.
  • Rup of 'Ra' (र के रूप):
    • रेफ (र्): Shift + Z (जैसे कर्म, धर्म)
    • पदेन (्र): Z key (जैसे प्रकाश, प्रेरणा)

5. 30-Day Step-by-Step Hindi Typing Roadmap

  1. Week 1 (Days 1–7): Home Row, Top Row aur Bottom Row ke characters bina keyboard dekhe yaad karein. Roz 30 minute single character drills karein.
  2. Week 2 (Days 8–14): Two & three letter simple words type karein: घर, पानी, कमल, समय, भारत, माता. Target 15 WPM.
  3. Week 3 (Days 15–21): Complex words aur Alt codes ki practice karein: संविधान, राष्ट्रपति, अंतर्राष्ट्रीय, विज्ञान. Target 25 WPM with 95% accuracy.
  4. Week 4 (Days 22–30): TypingDost Hindi Test par 10-minute full exam paragraphs timer ke sath solve karein. Target 35+ WPM.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Kya laptop keyboard se Hindi typing seekh sakte hain? ▾
Haan, basic practice laptop par ho sakti hai. Lekin Alt-codes ke liye USB Numeric Keypad zaroori hota hai, isliye exam se pehle ek standard desktop USB keyboard par practice zaroor karein.
Q: Remington Gail aur Inscript me kaunsa layout easy hai? ▾
Remington Gail beginners ke liye seekhna zyada intuitive aur easy hota hai kyunki iska typewriter layout natural rhythm deta hai.
